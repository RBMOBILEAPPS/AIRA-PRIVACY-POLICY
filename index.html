AIRA: Offline AI Companion

Privacy Policy · RBAPPS

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AIRA: Offline AI Companion (“AIRA”, “the App”) handles information. AIRA is developed and published by RBAPPS.

On-device AI: AIRA’s AI chat and memory features run on your device. Your chat messages and saved memories are not sent to RBAPPS or a cloud AI service for AI processing. Some optional features—such as model downloads, web searches, online content, and advertising—need an internet connection and may involve third-party services.

1. Information AIRA Handles

Information stored on your device

  • Chat messages and conversation history.
  • Companion profile details and locally generated memory/relationship summaries, where you use those features.
  • Saved articles, stories, links, settings, and notification history, as applicable.
  • The AI model file downloaded for on-device use.

This information is stored in the App’s private storage/database on your device. The current app database and preferences are not encrypted by a separate database-encryption layer; they rely on Android’s app sandbox and device security. Avoid entering passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive information into chats.

Information sent to third-party services when you use online features

When you choose online features, the relevant service may receive your search terms or request information, IP address, device/network information, and standard request headers. AIRA does not intentionally send your private chat history or saved memories as search queries.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Local AI chat: to generate responses on your device using the downloaded AI model.
  • Local memory: to personalize future conversations and keep information you choose to retain.
  • Model download: to download the model file from Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure.
  • Online search and content: to retrieve news, reference information, books, stories, and search results when requested.
  • Notifications: to schedule local companion notes or news digests if enabled.
  • Advertising and consent: to show ads and manage applicable privacy/consent choices.

3. Local AI, Chats, and Memories

AI inference is performed locally on your device. Chat content and memory records are not transmitted to RBAPPS servers or a cloud AI provider for generating responses. AIRA does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Chats and memories remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App. You can use the App’s conversation controls to clear chats and its memory controls to review, edit, or delete saved memories (where available). The App’s Android backup setting is disabled, so its app data is not intended to be backed up through Android’s standard cloud backup.

4. Online Features and Third-Party Services

These optional features require internet access. The App may contact the following services or service categories:

  • Hugging Face and its download/CDN infrastructure: to download the AI model. The provider receives ordinary download request/network information.
  • Wikipedia/Wikimedia: to look up reference information. Search terms or requested topics may be sent with the request.
  • DuckDuckGo: to retrieve web-search results. Search keywords may be sent.
  • News/RSS providers: including feeds or services such as The Hindu, Times of India, Google News, PIB, Gadgets360, ESPNcricinfo, and Jagran Josh, to retrieve news and digest content.
  • Project Gutenberg/Gutendex, Wikisource, and Open Library: to discover or retrieve public book/story information and content.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): to display ads and provide consent/privacy-choice controls.

These providers operate under their own privacy policies and practices. AIRA/RBAPPS does not control how third-party services process information they receive.

5. Advertising

AIRA uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner ads and may display ads in different areas of the App. AdMob may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), IP address, device information, approximate location inferred from IP, ad impressions, ad interactions, and diagnostics to provide, measure, and improve advertising and prevent fraud. AIRA does not intentionally provide chat messages or saved memories to AdMob for ad targeting.

Privacy/consent choices are available through the App’s “Ad Consent & Privacy Choices” setting, where supported. You can also manage advertising settings on your device or through Google’s privacy controls.

6. Analytics

RBAPPS does not intentionally integrate a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK in AIRA. Advertising SDKs may nevertheless collect their own operational, diagnostic, and advertising-related information as described by their providers.

7. Permissions

AIRA may request or use these Android permissions for the following purposes:

  • INTERNET: model downloads, online search/content, ads, and consent messages.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to check network connectivity.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show notifications if you enable them.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: to restore scheduled local notifications after a restart or app update.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: to schedule notifications at the intended times, where Android permits it.

AIRA does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location permissions as described in the current app audit.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local chat history, memories, and other app data are retained on your device until you delete them using the App’s controls or uninstall AIRA. You can delete conversations, manage or clear memories, and remove the downloaded model from the App’s settings/controls where available. Uninstalling the App removes its private local files and data. Third-party providers’ retention of data they receive is governed by their own policies.

9. Children’s Privacy and Audience

AIRA is not designed for or directed to children under 13, and RBAPPS does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App includes AI companion conversation and romantic-style content, so parents and guardians should use discretion when deciding whether it is appropriate for minors. If you believe a child has provided information to RBAPPS directly, contact us.

10. Security

App data is kept in Android’s private app storage and is protected by the platform’s app sandbox. However, the local database and preferences are not separately encrypted by AIRA. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock and do not share sensitive information in chats.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features, services, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published at the policy URL with an updated effective date.
